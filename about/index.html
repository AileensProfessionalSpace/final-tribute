About Claude Monet

Art is a passion that many people are interested in pursuing and they usually have an artist that pushed them to do so! Here is insight of a popular artist Claude Monet

Early Life

Oscar-Claude Monet was born on November 14, 1840, in the rue Lafitte in Paris, He moved and spent most of his childhood in Le Havre (In the Normandy region of northern France). His father wanted him to work for the family business but Claude Monet had different ideas on how he wanted to live his life. Monet wanted to become an artist so he began his career doing caricatures, around his early adult life he met a friend that encouraged him to quite spending time on caricatures and instead focus on landscapes. Later on in life Monet gave credit his friend Eugene Boudin for his artistic growth and his love of color

Family

During his art career you see a glimpse into his family life, his first wife Camille appears before her unfortunate passing due to an illness, along with Monets children. He had two biological children, Jean Monet and Michel Monet who were later accompanied by his step children from his second marriage. He remarried to Alice Hoschedé who had six children but one especially stood out because she cared for Monet in his later years and was also an impressionist painter (she also married his son).

His Career Journey

Despite the pressures as an artist to make historical/traditional artwork he chose instead to work alongside rebellious peers such as Pierre-Auguste Renoir and Camille Pissarro, this lead to multiple rejections in his independent exhibition but also growth among his artwork and later success. Throughout Monets attempts to provide for his family he struggled financially, his paintings rarely sold and they were solely relying on loans, eventually his artwork was marketed as impressionism to American buyers and he finally gained financial stability and well deserved success.
